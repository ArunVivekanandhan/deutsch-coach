## 🤖 KI Deutsch-Coach & Live Grammar Partner Features

* **Multi-AI Provider Integration**:
  * **DeepSeek AI** (`deepseek-chat`)
  * **OpenAI** (`gpt-4o`, `gpt-4o-mini`)
  * **Groq** (`llama-3.3-70b-versatile` — ultra-fast real-time speech response)
  * **Google Gemini** (`gemini-1.5-flash`)
  * **OpenRouter** (Universal proxy)
  * **Local Ollama** (100% private and offline on `localhost:11434`)
* **Real-Time Grammar Breakdown Under Every Message**:
  * 🟢 **Exact corrected sentence** with token diffs.
  * 📌 **Rule explanations** (cases, word order, prepositions).
  * 💡 **B1/B2 Idiomatic upgrades**.
  * ⭐ **Fehler-Tagebuch (Mistake Vault)**: Save corrections to review later.
* **4 AI Coach Personas**:
  * 👩‍🏫 *Frau Müller*: Patient teacher for A1–B1.
  * 👨‍🏫 *Herr Weber*: Goethe/telc/DTZ official examiner.
  * 🧑 *Lukas aus Berlin*: Casual tandem partner for everyday German.
  * 👨‍⚕️ *Dr. Hoffmann*: Professional workplace & medical communication coach.
* **Mobile-First & Voice-Ready**:
  * German Speech-to-Text (`de-DE`) microphone recognition.
  * German Speech Synthesis (TTS) with variable speed (0.85x, 1.0x, 1.15x).
